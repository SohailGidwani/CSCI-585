## Design decisions

* **Specialization/Generalization Strategy for Staff:** Created `STAFF` as a supertype containing common attributes (`monthly_salary`, personal info) and split role-specific logic into 1:1 subtypes `FRONT_OFFICE` and `MEDICAL_PROFESSIONAL`. `LICENSE` tracking is linked exclusively to `MEDICAL_PROFESSIONAL` to prevent non-medical personnel from having unnecessary license entries.
* **M:N Relationship Resolutions:**
* `PATIENT` and `INSURANCE_PROVIDER` are linked via `PATIENT_INSURANCE` (associative entity) to support patients carrying policy coverage from specific providers.
* `APPOINTMENT` and `CDT_CODE` are linked via `PATIENT_PROCEDURE` to track multiple CDT-coded treatments/surgeries/cleanings performed during a single appointment.
* `SUPPLY_ORDER` and `SUPPLY_ITEM` are linked via `SUPPLY_ORDER_ITEM` to track line items, quantities, and actual unit pricing per monthly restock order.


* **Omission of Stored Report Entities:** In compliance with database normalization best practices, daily schedule reports, daily billable income reports, and monthly P&L statements were deliberately **not modeled as stored entities**. Instead, all reports are dynamically derivable via SQL aggregate queries:
* *Daily Schedule:* Filter `APPOINTMENT` by `appointment_datetime` joined with `PATIENT`, `MEDICAL_PROFESSIONAL`, and `ROOM`.
* *Daily Income:* Sum `fee_charged` from `PATIENT_PROCEDURE` or payments received in `PATIENT_PAYMENT` and `INSURANCE_CLAIM` for a target date.
* *Monthly P&L / ROI Analytics:* Calculate total revenue (`PATIENT_PAYMENT` + `INSURANCE_CLAIM`) minus total expenses (`PAYROLL_RECORD` + `LEASE_PAYMENT` + `LOAN_PAYMENT` + `OPERATING_EXPENSE` + `SUPPLY_ORDER`).


* **Audit and Expense Associations:** Assigned `staff_id` foreign keys to `OPERATING_EXPENSE` and `SUPPLY_ORDER` so overhead spending and inventory purchasing can be traced directly to the staff member who logged or authorized the expense.
* **Patient Scheduling State:** Modeled `scheduling_state` as an attribute within `PATIENT` constrained to the five specified lifecycle values (`contacted`, `scheduled`, `recently visited`, `up for next visit`, `dormant`), enabling front-office staff to update patient status directly during outreach.
