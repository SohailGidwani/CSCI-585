You are a senior database designer. Design a conceptual ER diagram for a new dental practice, and output it as Mermaid erDiagram code.

BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

A dental practice that has not yet launched needs a database to run its operation. Model all seven areas below.

1. FINANCING. The owners take a $300,000 loan from a local bank, repaid with interest in installments spanning 10 years. Startup costs include furniture, dental equipment, software (scheduling, billing), supplies, and training.

2. STAFF. The practice hires front office workers, dental hygienists, dentists, periodontists, endodontists, orthodontists, and dental surgeons. The medical professionals (NOT the front office staff) hold state licenses with a license number, issue date, expiration date, and renewal status, and the business must track these so they stay current. Every staff member is paid a fixed monthly salary. There are no hourly rates and no overtime.

3. PATIENTS. About 100 patients. Each patient has a billing record holding insurance information (insurance provider name, insurance subscriber ID, coverage type such as "savings plan", amount of insurance coverage) and payment details (amount owed, amount paid by insurance, amount paid by the patient). Each patient also has a medical record consisting of procedures (e.g. teeth cleaning), treatments (e.g. gum disease) and surgeries (e.g. tooth extraction).

4. INSURANCE. Most patients carry dental insurance, so the staff maintains a list of insurance providers. Billing an insurance provider means submitting the patient's insurance subscriber ID, the type of treatment as a CDT procedure code (e.g. D1110), the treatment date, the amount billed/claimed, and the amount paid by the insurance company to the practice.

5. SCHEDULING. Hygienists and doctors (together called "doctors") serve patients in one of 8 rooms. Each appointment reserves one room for a specific doctor and a specific patient at a specific date and time. Front office staff contact patients to do the scheduling. At any moment a patient is in exactly one scheduling state: contacted, scheduled, recently visited, up for next visit, or dormant.

6. FACILITIES AND RECURRING COSTS. The owners lease a medical building near a major hospital on a monthly lease schedule. Supplies (needles, drugs, paper towels) are restocked monthly from vendors. There are other monthly operating costs: facilities cleaning, utilities, break room food.

7. REPORTING. The owners want to see the day's schedule each morning, an end-of-day report of the billable income generated by that day's services, and a monthly report of expenditure versus income so they can assess profit or loss. The data model must make these reports derivable.

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

- Cover all seven areas. Use these entities as a starting point, and rename, merge or split them if your design is better: STAFF, MEDICAL_PROFESSIONAL, FRONT_OFFICE_STAFF, LICENSE, SALARY_PAYMENT, PATIENT, PATIENT_SCHEDULING_STATE, APPOINTMENT, ROOM, PROCEDURE_CODE, TREATMENT, MEDICAL_RECORD, INSURANCE_PROVIDER, INSURANCE_POLICY, INSURANCE_CLAIM, INVOICE, PAYMENT, LOAN, LOAN_INSTALLMENT, LEASE, LEASE_PAYMENT, VENDOR, SUPPLY_ITEM, SUPPLY_ORDER, SUPPLY_ORDER_LINE, OPERATING_EXPENSE, STARTUP_COST.
- Every entity must take part in at least one relationship. Do not emit orphan entities.
- Give every entity a primary key marked PK, and mark every foreign key FK.
- Model the medical professional versus front office distinction as a specialization/subtype, and attach license tracking only to medical professionals.
- Model the five patient scheduling states explicitly, either as a lookup entity or as an enumerated attribute.
- Resolve every many-to-many relationship with an associative entity.
- State reasonable assumptions for anything the description leaves unspecified.

OUTPUT FORMAT, FOLLOW EXACTLY

First, output one fenced code block containing ONLY valid Mermaid erDiagram code. Write no text before the code block.
Then output a line containing three dashes.
Then output a short bulleted list of your assumptions and design decisions.

MERMAID SYNTAX RULES

- The first line inside the code block is: erDiagram
- Relationship lines look like: CUSTOMER ||--o{ ORDER : places
- Valid cardinality symbols are only ||, o|, |o, }o, o{, }| and |{.
- A relationship label must be a single word, or wrapped in double quotes if it has spaces.
- An attribute block looks like: ENTITY { then one attribute per line as "type name" with an optional PK, FK or UK at the end, then }
- Attribute types must be plain single words: string, int, float, decimal, date, datetime, boolean. Never write varchar(50), int(11), numeric(10,2) or anything containing parentheses or commas.
- Entity names and attribute names may contain only letters, digits and underscores. No spaces, no hyphens, no quotes, no commas, no parentheses.
- Do not put comments or explanatory text inside the code block.
